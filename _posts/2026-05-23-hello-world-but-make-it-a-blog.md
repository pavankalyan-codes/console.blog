# It finally compiled.

After years of writing code nobody reads and comments nobody asked for,
I decided to make it official — I now have a blog.

No stack traces. No merge conflicts. Just thoughts, shipped to production.

## What's coming

- **Deep dives** into engineering, focus, and how to actually get things done
- **War stories** from the trenches — bugs, breakdowns, and the occasional breakthrough
- **Hot takes** that are technically correct (the best kind of correct)
- **Tutorials** written by someone who Googled the same thing three times before writing them

## Why `console.blog()`?

Because `console.log()` was already taken.
And honestly, this whole blog is just me logging my thoughts somewhere
more permanent than a Slack message that disappears in 90 days.

Posts may contain strong opinions, weak coffee references,
and at least one unnecessary Breaking Bad analogy per article.
